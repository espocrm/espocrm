\# W5D2 - Business Process Mapping



\## Lead to Closure Process



1\. Lead is created.

2\. Lead is converted into an Account.

3\. Opportunity is created from the Lead.

4\. Opportunity is moved through sales stages.

5\. Activity/Task is created for follow-up.

6\. Opportunity can be closed as Won or Lost.



\## API Endpoints Inspected



1\. GET /api/v1/Lead

2\. GET /api/v1/Account

3\. GET /api/v1/Opportunity
