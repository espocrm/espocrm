# Week 5 Day 5 - AI Conversation Summarizer

## Objective

Understand how AI can summarize CRM conversations and improve customer support.

## Workflow

Customer Conversation
        ↓
Collect Conversation
        ↓
Send to AI Model
        ↓
Generate Summary
        ↓
Save Summary in CRM
        ↓
Display to User

## Benefits

- Saves time
- Quick conversation overview
- Better customer support
- Consistent summaries

## Example Prompt

Summarize the following customer conversation in 5 bullet points.

## Notes

Groq can be integrated using REST APIs. The CRM sends conversation text to the AI model and stores the returned summary.
