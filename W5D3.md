\# W5D3 - Enterprise CRM Architecture



\## Workflow

Workflows automate business rules based on triggers, conditions and actions.



\## BPM

BPM is used for more complex business processes with multiple steps and conditions.



\## Reporting

Reports help analyze CRM data and display useful business information.



\## Example Process

Lead → Account → Opportunity → Activity



\## Testing

EspoCRM was tested locally using Docker.

\## Key Difference



Workflow: Simple automation rules.

BPM: Complex multi-step business processes.

Report: Analysis and presentation of CRM data.
