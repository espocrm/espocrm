# W5D2 - Business Process Mapping

## Lead to Closure Flow

1. Lead Created
2. Lead Qualified
3. Opportunity Created
4. Account Created
5. Activity Scheduled
6. Opportunity Closed Won

## Business Process Mapping

Lead
↓
Opportunity
↓
Account
↓
Activity
↓
Closure

## API Inspection

### Endpoint 1
GET /api/v1/Lead

Purpose:
Retrieve lead records.

### Endpoint 2
GET /api/v1/Opportunity

Purpose:
Retrieve opportunity records.

### Endpoint 3
GET /api/v1/Account

Purpose:
Retrieve account records.

## Observations

- Lead is the starting point of the sales cycle.
- Opportunity tracks potential revenue.
- Account stores customer information.
- Activities track meetings, calls, and tasks.
